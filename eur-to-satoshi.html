What is a satoshi?

A satoshi is the smallest unit of Bitcoin. One bitcoin equals 100,000,000 satoshis. Converting euros to satoshis makes it easier to understand Bitcoin prices and values.

How does the EUR to satoshi conversion work?

The conversion is based on the live Bitcoin price in euros. As the price of Bitcoin changes, the number of satoshis per euro also changes.
