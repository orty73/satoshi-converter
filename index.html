What is a satoshi?

A satoshi (sat) is the smallest unit of Bitcoin. 1 sat = 0.00000001 BTC. This converter helps you estimate how many satoshis you get for Bitcoin or fiat currencies like EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, JPY, CNY and AUD.
