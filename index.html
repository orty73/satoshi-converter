What is a satoshi?

A satoshi (sat) is the smallest unit of Bitcoin. Instead of dealing with decimals like 0.00001234 BTC, satoshis make Bitcoin amounts easier to read and understand.

This website provides simple tools to convert BTC or fiat currencies into satoshis, using either fixed formulas or current market prices.
